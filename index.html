A GOD SIMULATOR

Humans, goblins and elves settle the land on their own. They raise houses, crown kings who age and die, forge alliances and go to war. You are their god, and every scar you leave on the world stays.

  • ✋ Hand of God: tear up houses, trees, people or earth and hurl them
  • ☄️ Meteors, ⚡ lightning, 🔥 fire: burn nations down
  • ⛰️ Raise and 🕳️ lower: reshape the land
  • 👁️ Possess: drop into a peasant and walk through the war you started
